# 摘要

智能交通与自动驾驶技术发展得越来越快，车辆感知技术也成了这个领域里的重点研究内容，而雾天、雨天、低光照这类恶劣天气，会让拍摄的图像质量变差，导致传统检测算法没法提取到有效特征，最终让检测效果出现明显下滑，针对这个实际问题，该项目搭建出一套依托图像增强技术的恶劣天气车辆感知系统，用它来改善恶劣环境下车辆检测精度偏低的现状。

本系统把YOLOv8模型当作核心检测结构，还搭配了多种图像优化手段，一用NAFNet网络完成图像去雾去雨操作，把画面原有的细节还原回来，二是靠MBLLEN算法优化低光照画面，提亮暗处画面的同时凸显出细节内容，另外会把CLAHE和形态学处理方式当作备用方案，保障特殊场景下的处理效果，系统内置的场景识别功能，能依托多项图像质量参数自主匹配对应的优化策略，不用额外训练专属模型，整体运算负担也更轻。

本系统采用前后端分离的开发架构，依靠推理优化工具把CPU设备的运行效率提了上去，能适配图片、视频、实时摄像三种识别模式，还能精准识别五类常用车辆，也可以借助数据库把检测数据长久留存下来。

经过多项实验验证，本系统能有效提升恶劣天气下的车辆检测精度，雾天能实现大概6.2%的精度提升，雨天的提升幅度能达到12.6%，低光照场景也能有4.2%的提升效果，整套系统能在普通CPU设备上稳定运行，不用搭载高性能显卡，还能有效降低部署成本和硬件使用门槛。

 

**关键词**:图像增强；YOLOv8；深度学习；车辆检测；恶劣天气

# **ABSTRACT**

Intelligent transportation and autonomous driving technology are developing faster and faster, vehicle perception technology has also become a key research content in this field, and foggy weather, rainy days, low light and other bad weather, will make the quality of the captured image worse, resulting in the traditional detection algorithm can not extract effective features, and finally the detection effect has declined significantly.

This system takes the YOLOv8 model as the core detection structure, and is also equipped with a variety of image optimization methods, one is to use the NAFNet network to complete the image defog and rain removal operation, and restore the original details of the picture, the other is to rely on the MBLLEN algorithm to optimize the low-light picture, brighten the dark picture while highlighting the details, and will use CLAH and morphological processing methods as backup solutions to ensure the processing effect in special scenarios, and the built-in scene recognition function of the system. It can rely on multiple image quality parameters to independently match the corresponding optimization strategy, without the need to train the exclusive model, and the overall computing burden is lighter.

The system adopts a development architecture with separation of front and rear ends, relying on reasoning optimization tools to improve the operating efficiency of CPU equipment, which can adapt to the three recognition modes of pictures, videos and real-time cameras, and can also accurately identify five types of commonly used vehicles, and can also use the database to retain the detection data for a long time.

After a number of experimental verifications, this system can effectively improve the accuracy of vehicle detection in bad weather, the accuracy can be improved by about 6.2% in foggy days, the improvement can reach 12.6% in rainy days, and the improvement effect in low-light scenes can also be 4.2%.

This study combines various image enhancement methods with intelligent detection algorithms. It has built a set of perception schemes with automatic scene adaptation capabilities, and has also completed the construction of a web application that can be used in land, and the relevant comparative tests of this study have also fully proved that the image pre-enhancement processing can indeed help the vehicle detection effect to be steadily improved.

 

**Keywords:** image enhancement, yolov8, deep learning, vehicle detection, adverse weather
